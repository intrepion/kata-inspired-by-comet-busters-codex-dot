# Vesper expedition
A cooperative expedition to clear the debris corridor around Vesper.

## Language
**Pilot**: A participant flying one ship. Avoid: user, actor.
**Hull**: One remaining attempt for a pilot, lost on an unprotected collision. Avoid: health point.
**Sector**: A field of comet fragments and raiders that must all be destroyed to continue. Avoid: level, round.
**Core**: The largest comet, which breaks into smaller fragments. Avoid: asteroid boss.
**Raider**: A hostile craft firing at pilots. Avoid: saucer.
**Energy**: A pilot's regenerating reserve for defensive abilities.
**Pulse**: A close-range disruption burst that breaks nearby threats.
**Blink**: A relocation to a clear position, with brief protection.
**Grace**: Temporary protection following a spawn or blink.
**Expedition**: Five sectors ending in corridor clearance or all pilots losing their hulls.
